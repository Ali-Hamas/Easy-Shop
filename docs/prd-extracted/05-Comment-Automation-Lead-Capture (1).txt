 Comment Automation and Lead
 Capture
Turns Facebook/Instagram comments like price, pp, details into inbox conversations and orders.
Why this exists
Comment sections create public interest. Fast private replies turn curiosity into sales.
Primary user
Owner, sales staff, buyer.
Business value
Increases lead capture from organic posts and ads.
Build priority
P1 - high attraction feature.
1. Page Goal
- Detect purchase-intent comments and reply/DM automatically.
- Capture leads from post comments, reels, and ad comments.
- Avoid spammy behavior that gets page restricted.
- Let staff review hot leads and negative comments.
2. How It Should Look
Post list: connected posts/reels with comment volume, leads, reply rate.
Rule builder: keyword, product match, public reply, private DM, delay.
Comment moderation queue: negative, spam, urgent, unanswered.
Lead board: interested, DM sent, replied, ordered.
3. Main Sections
Keyword rules
price, pp, inbox, available, size, location, custom Banglish/Roman Urdu words.
Product matching
Links post/reel to product or collection.
Public replies
Short safe reply under comment.
Private DM
Sends product details and order link.
Moderation
Hide spam, flag complaints, assign urgent.

5. Data Fields
- Post: platform, post ID, media, caption, linked product, status.
- Rule: keywords, language, delay, public reply, DM template, limit per hour.
- Lead: commenter ID, name, intent, product interest, DM status, order status.
- Moderation: sentiment, hidden flag, staff action, reason.
6. AI / Automation Behavior
- AI detects intent beyond exact keyword, but rules control sending.
- AI writes short natural public replies and product-specific DMs.
- AI flags angry comments for human, not auto-reply.
- AI avoids repeating same reply too many times under one post.
7. Empty, Error, and Safety States
- Empty state must show one clear next button, not a blank table.
- Every destructive action needs confirm: delete, cancel order, disconnect account, refund mark.
- If third-party API fails, keep local draft and show retry.
- AI must never invent price, stock, delivery charge, delivery date, or policy.
8. Acceptance Checklist
- Must respect Meta messaging policies.
- Rate-limit replies to avoid page restriction.
- Seller can preview exactly what buyer receives.
- Negative comments never get cheerful sales DM automatically.
9. Do Not Build Yet
- No mass scraping competitors' comments.
- No fake engagement tools.
- No auto-deleting all negative comments.
