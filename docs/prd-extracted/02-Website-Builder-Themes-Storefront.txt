 Storefront
Seller-facing builder and buyer-facing shopname.ourdomain.com storefront.
Why this exists
Website gives trust, order forms, product catalog, tracking, and a shareable link for social sellers.
Primary user
Seller and buyer.
Business value
Attracts signups. Converts social traffic into trackable orders.
Build priority
P0 - trust and checkout surface.
1. Page Goal
- Give every shop a mobile-first website with zero code.
- Let seller pick prebuilt design, colors, logo, banners, and sections.
- Make product discovery and COD checkout very fast.
- Support order tracking and customer trust elements.
2. How It Should Look
Builder: left settings panel, center mobile/desktop preview, top publish button.
Storefront: header, search, categories, hero/banner, product grid, cart, checkout.
Product page: photos, price, variant, stock, delivery charge, COD note, order button.
Tracking page: phone/order ID lookup with status timeline.
3. Main Sections
Theme picker
Prebuilt templates for fashion, cosmetics, gadgets, food, digital products.
Homepage sections
Hero banner, categories, best sellers, reviews, policy strip, footer.
Product page
Image gallery, variant selector, price, delivery, checkout CTA.
Checkout
Name, phone, address, area, delivery method, COD/advance.
Tracking
Order status for buyers without logging in.

5. Data Fields
- Theme: id, colors, font set, homepage sections, banner images.
- Storefront: title, meta description, logo, favicon, policy links.
- Checkout: required fields, delivery zones, payment methods, COD rules.
- SEO/share: product slug, social image, product description, schema basics.
6. AI / Automation Behavior
- AI can generate product descriptions, homepage banner copy, FAQs, and policy text.
- AI can suggest best template by business category.
- AI must not publish policy changes without seller approval.
- AI can compress/recommend image fixes, but original images stay stored.
7. Empty, Error, and Safety States
- Empty state must show one clear next button, not a blank table.
- Every destructive action needs confirm: delete, cancel order, disconnect account, refund mark.
- If third-party API fails, keep local draft and show retry.
- AI must never invent price, stock, delivery charge, delivery date, or policy.
8. Acceptance Checklist
- Store opens fast on low-end Android and 4G.
- Buyer can place order in under 60 seconds.
- Every product page has Messenger/WhatsApp fallback.
- No broken layout with long Bangla or Urdu product names.
9. Do Not Build Yet
- No drag-and-drop page builder in V1.
- No custom code injection for normal sellers.
- No 30 themes; start with 5 excellent themes.
