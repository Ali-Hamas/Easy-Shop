 Products and Inventory Management
Single source of truth for product data that AI, website, ads, orders, and courier use.
Why this exists
Bad product data makes AI lie and orders fail. Inventory must be simple but strict.
Primary user
Owner, product manager, staff.
Business value
Keeps AI useful, reduces overselling, enables ads/catalog and repeat orders.
Build priority
P0 - AI depends on it.
1. Page Goal
- Add and manage products, variants, stock, images, pricing, and delivery details.
- Support bulk import/export for sellers moving from spreadsheets.
- Track low stock and reserved stock from pending orders.
- Give AI approved product facts only.
2. How It Should Look
Products table: image, name, price, stock, status, category, actions.
Product editor: left fields, right live product card preview.
Inventory view: stock in hand, reserved, sold, returned, low stock.
Bulk import modal: upload, map columns, validate, confirm.
3. Main Sections
Product list
Search, filter, status, bulk update.
Product editor
Core info, variants, images, stock, SEO, AI notes.
Inventory ledger
Stock change history with reason and staff name.
Categories
Website navigation and AI recommendation grouping.
Import/export
Excel/CSV in and out.

5. Data Fields
- Product: name, SKU, category, description, price, compare price, cost optional.
- Inventory: stock, reserved, low stock threshold, warehouse optional V2.
- Variants: size, color, material, image, price override, stock.
- AI facts: selling points, who it is for, care instructions, policy exceptions.
6. AI / Automation Behavior
- AI answers only from active products and stock.
- AI recommends alternatives when requested item is out of stock.
- AI flags missing product fields that weaken replies.
- AI can create draft descriptions from image/name, owner approves.
7. Empty, Error, and Safety States
- Empty state must show one clear next button, not a blank table.
- Every destructive action needs confirm: delete, cancel order, disconnect account, refund mark.
- If third-party API fails, keep local draft and show retry.
- AI must never invent price, stock, delivery charge, delivery date, or policy.
8. Acceptance Checklist
- Stock never goes below zero unless seller enables backorder.
- Bulk import gives clear row-level errors.
- Order creation reserves stock; cancellation releases stock.
- Inventory ledger shows who changed what and when.
9. Do Not Build Yet
- No complex manufacturing/BOM inventory.
- No multi-warehouse in V1 unless one seller truly needs it.
- No barcode system until offline warehouse flow exists.
