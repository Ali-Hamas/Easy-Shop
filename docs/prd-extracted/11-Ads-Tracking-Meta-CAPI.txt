 Ads Tracking and Meta CAPI
Lightweight ads dashboard focused on correct tracking, product catalog, and delivered-order
revenue.
Why this exists
Sellers want ads. Full Ads Manager clone is too much; tracking and simple insights bring value faster.
Primary user
Owner, marketer.
Business value
Improves ad decisions and creates premium plan reason.
Build priority
P1/P2 - after orders are reliable.
1. Page Goal
- Connect Meta pixel/CAPI and product catalog.
- Send correct events from website and delivered orders.
- Show spend, orders, delivered revenue, ROAS, and creative winners.
- Avoid fake purchase tracking from unconfirmed COD orders.
2. How It Should Look
Ads overview: spend, orders, delivered revenue, ROAS, CPA, top products.
Tracking setup: pixel, CAPI, catalog sync, event test.
Campaign view: imported Meta campaign stats plus platform order data.
Attribution note: placed order vs delivered order clearly separated.
3. Main Sections
Tracking setup
Pixel/CAPI, event test, catalog feed.
Ad stats
Spend, clicks, messages, orders, delivered revenue.
Catalog sync
Products to Meta catalog with stock/price.
Delivered ROAS
Only delivered COD counted as real purchase value.
Recommendations
Simple AI suggestions, not auto-budget changes.

5. Data Fields
- Meta: page, ad account, pixel ID, catalog ID, access status.
- Events: view content, add to cart, initiate checkout, lead, purchase/delivered purchase.
- Campaign: spend, impressions, clicks, messages, orders, revenue.
- Attribution: click ID, channel, order ID, confidence.
6. AI / Automation Behavior
- AI explains ad performance in simple seller language.
- AI suggests pause/scale/test based on delivered revenue, not vanity metrics only.
- AI can create ad copy and audience ideas but owner launches manually.
- AI flags tracking errors and missing catalog fields.
7. Empty, Error, and Safety States
- Empty state must show one clear next button, not a blank table.
- Every destructive action needs confirm: delete, cancel order, disconnect account, refund mark.
- If third-party API fails, keep local draft and show retry.
- AI must never invent price, stock, delivery charge, delivery date, or policy.
8. Acceptance Checklist
- Never count every COD order as final purchase unless business chooses that metric.
- Show tracking health and last event time.
- If Meta API fails, keep local analytics visible.
- Ads advice must show data period used.
9. Do Not Build Yet
- No full campaign creation/editing in V1.
- No automatic budget changes.
- No guaranteed ROAS claims.
