 Delivery and Courier Integration
One-click booking, tracking, failed delivery handling, and courier performance view.
Why this exists
Courier work is where many Facebook sellers waste time and lose COD money.
Primary user
Owner, packer, support staff.
Business value
Sticky feature. Once courier flow works, shops do not want to leave.
Build priority
P0/P1 depending on available courier APIs.
1. Page Goal
- Connect courier partners per country.
- Book delivery from confirmed orders without retyping.
- Track parcel status and sync updates to buyer/order.
- Handle failed delivery, reschedule, return, and COD reconciliation.
2. How It Should Look
Courier settings: connected partners, pickup address, service type, default fees.
Booking queue: ready orders, validation issues, one-click book.
Tracking board: in transit, out for delivery, failed, delivered, returned.
Courier analytics: delivery rate, average time, RTO rate, COD pending.
3. Main Sections
Partner setup
Credentials, pickup, service area.
Booking
Create waybill from order.
Tracking sync
Pull status or receive webhook.
Failed delivery
Call/reschedule workflow.
Performance
Compare courier success and cost.

5. Data Fields
- Courier account: provider, credentials, pickup address, service type.
- Shipment: order ID, tracking ID, fee, COD amount, pickup date, status.
- Tracking event: status, timestamp, location, reason.
- Failed delivery: reason, buyer contact result, reschedule date.
6. AI / Automation Behavior
- AI detects bad address before booking.
- AI drafts buyer update messages based on tracking.
- AI flags courier partners with high failed delivery rates.
- AI suggests advance payment for risky areas/orders.
7. Empty, Error, and Safety States
- Empty state must show one clear next button, not a blank table.
- Every destructive action needs confirm: delete, cancel order, disconnect account, refund mark.
- If third-party API fails, keep local draft and show retry.
- AI must never invent price, stock, delivery charge, delivery date, or policy.
8. Acceptance Checklist
- No shipment without complete buyer address and phone.
- API downtime keeps retryable local booking draft.
- Manual courier mode exists for partners without API.
- Tracking status conflicts show source and last sync time.
9. Do Not Build Yet
- No own courier app.
- No rider assignment in V1.
- No international shipping unless seller base needs it.
