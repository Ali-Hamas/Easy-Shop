 Platform Overview and Roadmap
Master plan for an AI-first platform for Facebook, Instagram, and online sellers.
Why this exists
Keeps founder, designer, and developer aligned. The platform is not a generic Shopify clone. It is an AI sales operator for
social-commerce shops.
Primary user
Founder, product manager, designer, development team.
Business value
Defines what attracts signups and what keeps shops paying monthly.
Build priority
Read first. All other PDFs follow this direction.
1. Page Goal
- Main promise: website + AI inbox + inventory + order + delivery + ads tools in one dashboard.
- Serve sellers doing 0-200 COD orders per day from Facebook and Instagram.
- Make AI inbox and order confirmation the hero, because missed messages and manual orders cost money.
- Keep V1 focused: fast setup, reliable AI replies, courier booking, COD tracking, customer retention.
2. How It Should Look
Left sidebar: Dashboard, Inbox, Orders, Products, Website, Delivery, Payments, Marketing, Ads, AI Studio, Settings.
Top bar: shop switcher, connection status, alerts, help, owner profile.
Home dashboard: today sales, pending chats, pending orders, courier issues, low stock.
Global AI command bar: 'Ask AI to do work' visible on every page but never auto-runs risky actions.
3. Main Sections
Hero value
AI replies to customer messages, recommends products, confirms order, and sends order to courier.
Website
Each shop gets shopname.ourdomain.com with catalog, checkout, tracking, and trust signals.
Operations
Inventory, orders, courier, payments, staff roles, and analytics.
Growth
Ad maker, customer CRM, coupons, broadcasts, delivered-order tracking.

5. Data Fields
- Shop: name, subdomain, owner, country, currency, language, timezone.
- Connections: Facebook page, Instagram account, WhatsApp number, courier accounts.
- Commercials: plan, monthly fee, order limit, AI message limit, staff seats.
- Compliance: privacy policy, return policy, COD rules, message consent logs.
6. AI / Automation Behavior
- AI reads only approved product data, policies, order status, and customer chat history.
- AI actions have levels: suggest, draft, ask approval, execute.
- High-risk actions need human approval: cancel order, mark paid, refund, bulk message, courier booking.
- AI must support Bangla, Banglish, English, Roman Urdu, and simple Urdu where possible.
7. Empty, Error, and Safety States
- Empty state must show one clear next button, not a blank table.
- Every destructive action needs confirm: delete, cancel order, disconnect account, refund mark.
- If third-party API fails, keep local draft and show retry.
- AI must never invent price, stock, delivery charge, delivery date, or policy.
8. Acceptance Checklist
- Can a seller launch basic shop in under 5 minutes?
- Can a customer go from message to confirmed order without staff typing details twice?
- Can owner see money, orders, chats, courier issues on one screen?
- Can staff undo or review AI work?
9. Do Not Build Yet
- Do not build full Shopify app store in V1.
- Do not build full Meta Ads Manager clone in V1.
- Do not build custom courier fleet.
