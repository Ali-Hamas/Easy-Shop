 AI Ad Creative Studio
Creates ad copy, product hooks, image text ideas, video scripts, and campaign angles.
Why this exists
Small sellers need better ads but cannot hire copywriters and designers every week.
Primary user
Owner, marketer, content creator.
Business value
Paid add-on and high attraction feature.
Build priority
P1 - can launch before full ads manager.
1. Page Goal
- Generate ad ideas from selected products.
- Create multiple copy variations for Facebook/Instagram.
- Support Bangla, Banglish, English, Roman Urdu.
- Create briefs for image/video creatives.
2. How It Should Look
Input panel: choose product, objective, offer, language, tone.
Output tabs: primary text, headline, short hook, video script, image text, CTA.
Variation grid: 10 hooks, 5 captions, 5 offers.
Save/export: copy, download brief, send to campaign draft.
3. Main Sections
Product selector
Pulls facts, price, benefits, reviews.
Offer builder
Discount, bundle, free delivery, limited stock.
Copy generator
Captions, headlines, CTAs, hooks.
Video script
15s/30s reel script with shot list.
Brand memory
Tone, banned claims, target audience.

5. Data Fields
- Creative request: product, objective, offer, audience, language, tone.
- Outputs: caption, headline, CTA, hook, script, visual notes.
- Brand rules: banned words, claims, preferred terms, emojis yes/no.
- Performance link: which creative was used in campaign and outcome later.
6. AI / Automation Behavior
- AI uses only real product facts and seller-approved claims.
- AI warns against medical, financial, or unrealistic claims.
- AI creates short mobile-first hooks.
- AI learns from saved winning copy but never copies competitor text.
7. Empty, Error, and Safety States
- Empty state must show one clear next button, not a blank table.
- Every destructive action needs confirm: delete, cancel order, disconnect account, refund mark.
- If third-party API fails, keep local draft and show retry.
- AI must never invent price, stock, delivery charge, delivery date, or policy.
8. Acceptance Checklist
- Generated ads need seller review before use.
- Claims like guaranteed cure, instant fairness, impossible income are blocked/warned.
- Outputs should fit Meta ad text constraints where possible.
- Saved assets are searchable by product and date.
9. Do Not Build Yet
- No full video generation in V1 unless external tool is already available.
- No auto-posting ads without review.
- No competitor ad copying.
