 Analytics Dashboard
Simple business intelligence for sales, operations, customers, delivery, and AI performance.
Why this exists
Owners need to know what happened today and what to fix tomorrow.
Primary user
Owner and manager.
Business value
Helps sellers see value and justify monthly payment.
Build priority
P1 - after reliable event data.
1. Page Goal
- Show sales, orders, conversion, delivery, RTO, product, channel, and AI metrics.
- Separate placed orders, confirmed orders, shipped orders, delivered revenue.
- Surface problems: low stock, high return product, courier delay, poor reply time.
- Make reports exportable.
2. How It Should Look
Dashboard top: date range, gross sales, delivered revenue, orders, RTO, AI saved time.
Charts: sales by day, orders by source, status funnel, top products.
Problem cards: low stock, high RTO, failed delivery, unanswered chats.
Report tabs: sales, products, channels, delivery, AI, staff.
3. Main Sections
Sales metrics
Placed, confirmed, shipped, delivered, cancelled, returned.
Product metrics
Best sellers, low stock, return-heavy products.
Channel metrics
Facebook, Instagram, website, manual.
Delivery metrics
Courier success, average delivery time, RTO.
AI metrics
Auto replies, handoffs, confirmed orders, confidence issues.

5. Data Fields
- Metrics: order counts, revenue types, delivery statuses, channel/source.
- Time: created, confirmed, shipped, delivered, returned timestamps.
- AI: message count, automation rate, handoff rate, correction rate.
- Staff: assigned chats, confirmed orders, response time.
6. AI / Automation Behavior
- AI explains why sales changed using available data.
- AI recommends low-risk actions: restock, follow up, update ad copy, pause bad product.
- AI can generate weekly report summary.
- AI avoids fake certainty when data is incomplete.
7. Empty, Error, and Safety States
- Empty state must show one clear next button, not a blank table.
- Every destructive action needs confirm: delete, cancel order, disconnect account, refund mark.
- If third-party API fails, keep local draft and show retry.
- AI must never invent price, stock, delivery charge, delivery date, or policy.
8. Acceptance Checklist
- Metrics define placed vs delivered clearly.
- Charts work with zero/low data and explain what to do next.
- Exports match visible filters.
- No vanity-only dashboard; every chart links to action.
9. Do Not Build Yet
- No complex BI builder in V1.
- No predictive forecasting until enough historical data.
- No fake AI insights without data backing.
