 Team, Settings, Billing, and Security
Controls staff access, shop settings, language, billing plans, connected accounts, and safety.
Why this exists
Small teams share passwords too often. The platform needs simple roles and clear billing.
Primary user
Owner, admin, staff.
Business value
Enables paid plans, team growth, and trust.
Build priority
P0/P1 - basic version required before launch.
1. Page Goal
- Manage staff roles and permissions.
- Control shop settings, policies, integrations, language, notifications.
- Show billing plan, usage, invoices, and upgrade path.
- Protect account with audit logs and secure access.
2. How It Should Look
Settings tabs: Profile, Shop, Team, Integrations, AI, Notifications, Billing, Security.
Team table: name, role, status, last active, permissions, actions.
Billing page: current plan, usage bars, invoices, payment method, upgrade.
Audit log: user/action/date/object/status.
3. Main Sections
Team roles
Owner, admin, sales, packer, marketer, accountant.
Permissions
Page-level and action-level controls.
Integrations
Meta, courier, payment, WhatsApp/SMS.
Billing
Plan limits, invoices, usage, upgrade/downgrade.
Security
Password/OTP, sessions, audit logs.

5. Data Fields
- User: name, email/phone, role, status, last active.
- Role: permissions for inbox, orders, products, payments, exports, billing.
- Billing: plan, limits, usage, invoices, payment status.
- Security: sessions, login events, audit actions, integration tokens.
6. AI / Automation Behavior
- AI respects user permissions before answering or acting.
- AI explains plan limit and suggests upgrade only when relevant.
- AI can help configure policies but needs owner approval.
- AI flags suspicious staff actions from audit patterns later.
7. Empty, Error, and Safety States
- Empty state must show one clear next button, not a blank table.
- Every destructive action needs confirm: delete, cancel order, disconnect account, refund mark.
- If third-party API fails, keep local draft and show retry.
- AI must never invent price, stock, delivery charge, delivery date, or policy.
8. Acceptance Checklist
- Owner cannot remove own access without transfer flow.
- Payment/export permissions are restricted by default.
- Disconnecting Meta/courier shows affected features.
- Billing usage updates clearly and avoids surprise charges.
9. Do Not Build Yet
- No enterprise SSO in V1.
- No custom role builder until fixed roles fail.
- No complicated subscription proration rules early.
