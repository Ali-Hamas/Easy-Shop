 AI Inbox for Messenger and Instagram
The main money feature: AI reads customer messages and helps sell, support, and confirm orders.
Why this exists
Social sellers lose sales because replies are slow and staff copy-paste answers. This fixes that.
Primary user
Owner, sales staff, buyer.
Business value
Strongest subscription driver. Saves staff time and increases confirmed orders.
Build priority
P0 - hero feature.
1. Page Goal
- Unify Messenger and Instagram DMs in one inbox.
- AI replies using product, inventory, policy, and customer history.
- Support modes: suggest-only, auto-reply low-risk, human approve for orders.
- Convert chat into draft/confirmed order without retyping.
2. How It Should Look
Left: conversation list with channel, unread, AI status, intent, order risk.
Center: chat thread with buyer messages, AI drafts, staff replies.
Right: customer card, cart/order draft, product suggestions, policy snippets.
Top: filters for unread, needs human, order intent, complaint, VIP.
3. Main Sections
Unified inbox
All customer DMs with search and filters.
AI reply panel
Shows draft, confidence, source facts, approve/edit/send.
Order capture
AI extracts name, phone, address, product, variant, quantity.
Human handoff
Assign to staff, pause AI, internal notes.
Conversation memory
Customer order history and previous issues.

5. Data Fields
- Conversation: channel, page, buyer ID, language, intent, status, assigned staff.
- Message: sender, text, attachments, timestamp, AI confidence, source references.
- Customer: name, phone, address, tags, order count, return history.
- Order draft: items, variant, quantity, delivery area, payment method, notes.
6. AI / Automation Behavior
- AI classifies intent: price, availability, order, complaint, delivery status, return, spam.
- AI asks one question at a time for missing order details.
- AI cites internal source: product, policy, order, delivery status.
- AI escalates low-confidence, angry customer, refund, legal, abuse, or VIP messages.
7. Empty, Error, and Safety States
- Empty state must show one clear next button, not a blank table.
- Every destructive action needs confirm: delete, cancel order, disconnect account, refund mark.
- If third-party API fails, keep local draft and show retry.
- AI must never invent price, stock, delivery charge, delivery date, or policy.
8. Acceptance Checklist
- AI must not confirm unavailable stock.
- AI must not promise exact delivery date unless courier status confirms it.
- Staff can see why AI answered that way.
- Every auto-sent message can be found in audit log.
9. Do Not Build Yet
- No voice-call AI in V1.
- No fully autonomous refund/cancel actions.
- No replying from personal staff Facebook accounts.
