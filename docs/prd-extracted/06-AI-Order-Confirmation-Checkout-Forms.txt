 AI Order Confirmation and Checkout
 Forms
Converts chat interest into clean orders with name, phone, address, product, payment, and risk
checks.
Why this exists
Most small sellers lose money through wrong addresses, fake orders, and manual confirmation chaos.
Primary user
Buyer, sales staff, owner.
Business value
Direct revenue feature: more confirmed orders, fewer returns.
Build priority
P0 - order quality.
1. Page Goal
- Let AI collect missing order details naturally inside chat.
- Offer checkout link when chat becomes messy.
- Validate phone, address, area, stock, payment method, and duplicate orders.
- Use risk rules before confirming COD orders.
2. How It Should Look
Chat order card: items, buyer info, delivery, payment, risk score, confirm button.
Checkout form: product summary, name, phone, district/city, address, COD/advance.
Risk banner: safe, review, require advance.
Confirmation screen: order ID, tracking expectation, contact options.
3. Main Sections
Order extraction
Reads details from conversation and fills order card.
Checkout form
Short mobile form shareable in DM.
Validation
Phone, duplicate, stock, delivery zone, payment rules.
Risk scoring
Return history, area, COD amount, incomplete address.
Confirmation
Creates order and sends buyer receipt.

5. Data Fields
- Buyer: name, phone, alternate phone, address, city/district/area.
- Order: item, variant, quantity, price snapshot, discount, delivery charge, total.
- Payment: COD, advance, paid amount, transaction ID, proof image.
- Risk: score, reason, previous returns, duplicate order check.
6. AI / Automation Behavior
- AI extracts fields but shows confidence for each field.
- AI asks for missing data in buyer language.
- AI decides safe/review/advance using rules, not free imagination.
- AI summarizes order before final confirm.
7. Empty, Error, and Safety States
- Empty state must show one clear next button, not a blank table.
- Every destructive action needs confirm: delete, cancel order, disconnect account, refund mark.
- If third-party API fails, keep local draft and show retry.
- AI must never invent price, stock, delivery charge, delivery date, or policy.
8. Acceptance Checklist
- No order confirm without phone, address, item, quantity, and price.
- Duplicate buyer/order warning before confirmation.
- If AI extracts wrong address, staff can edit before confirm.
- Buyer gets clear cancellation/reschedule policy.
9. Do Not Build Yet
- No automatic high-value COD confirmation without review.
- No full fraud scoring marketplace in V1.
- No forced account creation for buyer.
