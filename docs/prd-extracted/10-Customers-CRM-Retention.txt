 Customers, CRM, Coupons, and
 Retention
Turns one-time buyers into repeat buyers through customer history, tags, coupons, and safe
broadcasts.
Why this exists
New customers are expensive. Repeat customers bring profit.
Primary user
Owner, marketing staff, support staff.
Business value
Creates recurring value after first order. Drives upsell plans.
Build priority
P1 - growth feature.
1. Page Goal
- Store buyer profile, order history, chat history, tags, and return behavior.
- Segment customers by product, value, city, repeat, inactive, risky.
- Create coupons and campaigns without spamming.
- Track repeat purchase and campaign revenue.
2. How It Should Look
Customer list: name, phone, orders, total spent, last order, tags, risk.
Customer profile: timeline, orders, chats, addresses, notes, coupons.
Segments: filters with saved audience count.
Campaign composer: audience, message, coupon, channel, preview, schedule.
3. Main Sections
Customer database
All buyers and leads in one place.
Tags/segments
VIP, frequent return, Eid buyer, skincare buyer, city.
Coupons
Discount codes for website/order forms.
Broadcasts
WhatsApp/SMS/Messenger-safe messages.
Retention analytics
Repeat rate and campaign revenue.

5. Data Fields
- Customer: name, phone, addresses, channels, language, tags, risk.
- History: orders, chats, returns, complaints, payments, campaign clicks.
- Coupon: code, type, value, min order, expiry, usage limit.
- Consent: channel, opt-in source, timestamp, opt-out status.
6. AI / Automation Behavior
- AI suggests segments: likely repeat buyers, high-value buyers, inactive buyers.
- AI writes campaign copy in Bangla/Banglish/English/Roman Urdu.
- AI recommends product upsells from purchase history.
- AI must respect opt-out and channel policy.
7. Empty, Error, and Safety States
- Empty state must show one clear next button, not a blank table.
- Every destructive action needs confirm: delete, cancel order, disconnect account, refund mark.
- If third-party API fails, keep local draft and show retry.
- AI must never invent price, stock, delivery charge, delivery date, or policy.
8. Acceptance Checklist
- Broadcasts require preview and final confirmation.
- Opt-out customers must never receive campaigns.
- Duplicate customer merge must not lose order history.
- Coupon abuse rules show usage limit and expiry clearly.
9. Do Not Build Yet
- No spam tools.
- No cold lead scraping.
- No complicated loyalty points in V1.
